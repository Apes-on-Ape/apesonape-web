# Flappy Ape 2.0 - Enhanced Edition

A dramatically improved version of the classic Flappy Bird game featuring progressive difficulty, power-ups, special effects, and much more!

## 🎮 New Features

### 🔥 Progressive Difficulty System
- **10 Difficulty Levels**: The game gets progressively harder as your score increases
- **Dynamic Adjustments**: Pipe speed, gap size, and gravity change automatically
- **Special Pipe Patterns**: 
  - Normal pipes (Levels 1-2)
  - Wave patterns (Level 3)
  - Moving pipes (Level 4)
  - Double pipes (Level 5)
  - Zigzag patterns (Level 6)
  - Narrow passages (Level 7)
  - Chaos mode (Level 8)
  - Extreme patterns (Level 9)
  - Nightmare mode (Level 10)

### ⚡ Power-Up System
- **Shield (🛡️)**: 8-second immunity to pipe collisions
- **Slow Motion (⏰)**: 4-second time dilation effect
- **Double Jump (⬆️)**: 10-second ability to jump twice in mid-air
- **Coin Magnet (🧲)**: 6-second automatic coin collection
- **Tiny Ape (🔸)**: 7-second 50% size reduction for easier navigation

### 💰 Coin Collection System
- **Dynamic Spawning**: Coins appear randomly in pipe gaps
- **Streak Bonuses**: Consecutive coin collection multiplies rewards
- **Magnetic Collection**: Coin Magnet power-up attracts nearby coins
- **Visual Feedback**: Particle effects and pulsing animations

### 🌪️ Environmental Effects
- **Wind System**: Dynamic wind forces affect ape movement
- **Visual Wind Indicator**: Shows wind direction and strength
- **Background Transitions**: Colors change dynamically during gameplay
- **Animated Clouds**: Moving cloud effects when background images fail to load

### ✨ Visual Enhancements
- **Particle System**: Explosion effects for scoring, power-ups, and collisions
- **Screen Shake**: Impact feedback for difficulty increases and collisions
- **Glowing Effects**: Special pipe patterns have visual indicators
- **Enhanced Bird Rendering**: Improved fallback graphics with eyes and gradients
- **Shield Visual**: Blue glow effect when shield is active

### 🎨 UI Improvements
- **Real-time Stats**: Live display of score, coins, and difficulty level
- **Power-up Indicators**: Progress bars showing active power-up duration
- **Color-coded Difficulty**: Difficulty display changes color based on level
- **Responsive Design**: Optimized for mobile, tablet, and desktop
- **Enhanced Animations**: Smooth transitions and hover effects

## 🎯 Gameplay Features

### 🎮 Enhanced Controls
- **Double Jump**: Press jump again while falling with Double Jump power-up
- **Precise Collision**: Improved collision detection with padding
- **Wind Resistance**: Navigate changing wind conditions
- **Shield Protection**: Temporary invulnerability periods

### 📊 Scoring System
- **Base Points**: 10 points per pipe successfully passed
- **Coin Values**: 1 point per coin (2x with streak bonus)
- **Difficulty Scaling**: Higher difficulty levels provide greater challenge
- **High Score Tracking**: Personal best scores saved automatically

### 🌟 Achievement System (Ready for Implementation)

- **Sky Warrior**: Score 100 points - Unlock Gold Ape
- **Legend**: Score 200 points - Unlock Diamond Ape
- **Impossible**: Score 500 points - Unlock Rainbow Ape
- **Godlike**: Score 1000 points - Unlock Cosmic Ape

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. **Starting**: Click "Play Game" or press SPACE to begin
2. **Flying**: Press SPACE, click, or tap to make the ape jump
3. **Survival**: Navigate through pipes while collecting coins and power-ups
4. **Power-ups**: Collect glowing orbs for temporary abilities
5. **Progression**: Survive longer to reach higher difficulty levels
6. **Mastery**: Use power-ups strategically to achieve high scores
